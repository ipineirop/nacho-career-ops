Ten rules Voice

The system.

One palette, two modes, three typefaces, ten rules. Every component on every screen of LeadMe lives inside this book. If something isn't here, it isn't in the product yet.

01Color

One accent. Petrol on light, sky-blue on dark — same role, mode-mapped. One signal. Burnt amber on light, saffron on dark, used only for under-market and stale-state warnings. Pulled deliberately away from safety-orange, toward saffron / brass.

★ Rule on the signal color

No warn-vs-flag hierarchy by hue. One signal color, two intensities. Filled pill = serious ("16% under market," "level mismatch"). Outlined pill = soft ("stale 5d," "fields skipped"). Pill style does the hierarchy. The color stays consistent.

Density follows surface. Brief/Evaluate breathe (22–32px padding). Tracker tightens (10–14px row padding). The product feels editorial where it reads, tabular where it operates.

Radius · 10 / 999 (px). Two values, no in-between.

10 PX
10px — cards, score chips, modals, salary bands, lead cards, avatar squares
999 PX

No shadows. Ever. Hierarchy comes from borders, radius, density — never blur. A dropped shadow is the design failing.

15The ten rules

Restrictions are the brand. If the system can't say it, the design is wrong before the system is.

01 One accent. One signal. Petrol/Sky for action. Amber for warning. That's the entire chromatic palette. Third color = wrong design.
02 Two radii. Forever. 10px for cards, score chips, modals, salary bands. 999px for pills, buttons, people avatars. Nothing else exists.
03 No shadows. Ever. Hierarchy comes from borders, radius, density — never blur. A dropped shadow is the design failing.
04 One spacing scale. 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 px. Nothing in between. Ever.
05 Avatars are squares. Companies: 10–12px radius squares, accent-soft, two-letter initials. People: 999px circles. Never photos. Never logos in MVP.
06 Numbers wear a uniform. All scores, percentages, currency, dates render in Geist Mono. Labels uppercase. The "receipts" feel comes from this consistency.
07 Serif is a moment. Fraunces appears at exactly four sizes: 22 (verdict), 28 (brief pick), 44 (brief hero), 68 (doc hero). Never paragraphs.
08 Density follows surface. Brief / Evaluate breathe — 22–32px padding. Tracker tightens — 10–14px row padding. Editorial where it reads, tabular where it operates.
09 Score chips are iconic. Always 56×56 (verdict) or 44×44 (list). Always 10–14px radius. Never a circle. Never anywhere else.
10 No icons until earned. Words over glyphs. If a label needs an icon to be understood, the label is wrong. Iconography only after every screen ships text-first.

16Voice

The product is a candid, calm advisor — never desperate, never breathless. It speaks like someone who's read your CV and respects your time.

Do

"Kavak just opened your seat."
"Worth your time — with caveats."
'Competitive' is doing a lot of work in that DM. Market for Head of Ops at a Series C MX fintech with your scope: $1.9–2.4M MXN + 0.1–0.25% equity.

Don't

✕ "🎉 We found 7 amazing opportunities for you!"
✕ "Don't miss out on these matches — apply now!"
✕ "Score: 82/100 — Good match"
✕ "Time to take your career to the next level."
Tone — candid, dry, informed. Speaks to a Director, not at a user.
Diction — short clauses. Em-dashes. Italics for emphasis sparingly. No exclamation marks.
Honesty — when something's unclear, say so. "Medium confidence · 2 sources" beats false certainty.
Never — gamification, urgency tactics, emoji, sales-y CTAs, "we" as in the product.
