Adaptations
Sidebar → bottom tab bar. Five tabs: Discover · Home · ✦ Evaluate · Tracker · More. The center tab is the accent-filled CTA, slightly elevated. Always visible.
Tracker table → card list. One card per role, score chip + role title + status footer. Same data, no horizontal scroll.
Pick rows → stacked cards. Title first, salary band inline, actions full-width buttons.
Filters → horizontal scroll pills. Bleeds off the right edge to signal more.
Tap targets ≥ 44px. Buttons grow to 11px vertical padding, full-width by default on mobile.
Newly added · v0.2
06 · Report. The most read-heavy screen. Sticky compact header (score + company), TOC pills, blocks A–E (role / fit / comp / gaps / reply). Pull quote in Fraunces italic for the editorial moment. Disagree row at the bottom — one tap, opens an annotation flow (the brief's component-level transparency commitment).
07 · Mode picker. Pulled out of the multi-step form per the brief's note. Three full-tap cards, not radios. Leverage is pre-selected with a one-line rationale built from the CV — calm default, override anytime.
08 · Spanish at parity. Same Discover surface, ~18% longer. Albert Sans handles diacritics cleanly. Tab labels translate (Hallazgos · Inicio · Evaluar · Tracker · Más). The voice card shows EN/ES copy pairs side-by-side — Labra never code-switches mid-sentence.
What didn't change
The 10 rules. Two radii (10/999), one spacing scale, no shadows, no icons earned yet, score chips iconic, serif moments preserved (smaller sizes: 18–32px instead of 22–48px), all numerics in Geist Mono uppercase. The signal color stays burnt amber. Pin still off-band. Fit bars still A–F with signal-amber fill on D grades.
