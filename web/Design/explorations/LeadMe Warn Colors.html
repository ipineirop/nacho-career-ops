Warning colors.

look & feel · palette exploration · warn × flag pairs
Locked palette: Petrol light + Midnight dark (toggleable). Open question — what color flags "under market", "stale 5d", "level mismatch"? Six options below, each rendered on both modes against the same Evaluate verdict.
where these colors live
Two signal levels, distinct from the petrol accent (which is positive / neutral). They appear in three spots only:
  • Salary band pin — when an offer falls below or above the user's band
  • Pill / chip — "≈ 16% under market" · "stale 5d" · "level mismatch"
  • Fit-bar fill — for the comp / level / domain dimensions that score poorly (D/F)
We're not recoloring whole cards or backgrounds. These are surgical accents, used on < 5% of pixels.

My read

Option 03 (Red only) or Option 04 (Cool & Sharp).

Option 03 · Red only is the most restrained, most product-confident answer. The product is candid; let one color do the work. Two-tier hierarchy comes from filled vs outlined intensity. Most "Linear" of the bunch.
Option 04 · Cool & Sharp wins if you want every color in the system to live in the cool half of the wheel — it's the most disciplined option and reads grown-up across both modes.
I'd skip 01 (muddy on petrol), 05 (too generic), and 06 (purple-adjacent). 02 is a strong sleeper — quiet enough to almost qualify as "single-signal with a hint of dimmer warn."
