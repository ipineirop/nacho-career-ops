Look & feel

v0.1 · 3 directions · for review
Translating the wireframes into a voice. Sweet spot between Linear-style pro tool and warm-companion trust — calibrated 4/10 on the warmth scale, professional first. No oranges. No purples. Sketchy hand-feel retired. Uber Eats restaurant app as the gravitational anchor.
the brief
LeadMe is a quiet, trusted advisor — not a job board, not a CRM, not a chatbot. The product surface should feel like a sharp desk tool that respects your time: confident typography, generous spacing, a single decisive accent color, and editorial moments for the Brief itself.

DIRECTION 01

Ledger

A calm, slightly editorial pro tool. Cream-paper neutrals updated to feel modern — not vintage. A single deep forest green as the load-bearing accent. Serif headlines for the Brief; clean sans for everything else. Reads as the careful tool a finance ops director would actually want to open in the morning.

My recommendation

Direction 02 · Move, with one borrow from Ledger. The Uber Eats DNA you cited is real and the strategy doc keeps using words like "candid," "honest," "decisive" — Move is the only direction that looks like those words. But we should reserve a serif (Fraunces or similar) for the Brief headlines specifically — that's where the product earns its "considered companion" trust signal that pure sans struggles to deliver. Everything else stays Move.
