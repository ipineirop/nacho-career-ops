Move, in blues.

look & feel v0.2 · Move + Revolut DNA · three palettes
You picked Move. We're keeping its bones — confident sans, generous spacing, decisive single-accent — and swapping the emerald for cool blue / dark teal with a touch of Revolut's premium-fintech polish. Three sub-variants to land the final tone.
what's locked in
Same skeleton across all three: Albert Sans for body and UI (humanist geometric, Move-family), Geist for confident display moments, Fraunces reserved exclusively for editorial Brief headlines, Geist Mono for numerics. Decisive primary actions, rounded corners (10–14px), generous whitespace.
no greens no oranges no purples Revolut polish Uber-Eats density premium fintech, not flashy

Side-by-side trade-offs

Marine "premium fintech, decisive." Most familiar. Fastest user trust. Highest risk of looking like every other neo-bank. Hardest to make distinctive.
Petrol "the advisor you keep open." Most differentiated. Reads grown-up rather than startup-y. Slightly less immediate "tech" signal — solved with type weight and density.
Midnight "premium tier, focused." Most cinematic. Strongest brand statement. Riskiest at first-impression for non-dark-mode users. Best as a Pro mode later, not the v1 default.
recommendation

Push Petrol — keep Midnight in the back pocket as a future "Pro" mode.

Marine is what every fintech competitor would do — fast to ship, slowest to remember. Petrol is the right grown-up answer for an audience that already has leverage and isn't looking for shiny. Midnight is the strongest future mode for daily-use surfaces (Brief, Evaluate, Tracker) — perfect for a "Pro / focus" toggle once the product earns its premium tier. Petrol as the launch palette. Midnight as the earned reward.
