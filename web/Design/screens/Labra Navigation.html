The shape of every screen. La forma de cada pantalla.

Labra has three destinations in v1 — Evaluate, Tracker, Settings — and two viewports that behave differently. Desktop gets a persistent 240 px side rail; mobile gets a slim top bar + bottom tab bar. Same destinations, different geometry. No hamburger, no drawer, no side-rail-on-mobile. This document is the source of truth for both. Labra tiene tres destinos en v1 — Evaluar, Tracker, Ajustes — y dos viewports que se comportan distinto. Escritorio recibe un side rail persistente de 240 px; móvil recibe una barra superior delgada + tab bar inferior. Mismos destinos, geometría distinta. Sin hamburguesa, sin drawer, sin side-rail en móvil. Este documento es la fuente de verdad para ambos.

Persistent left rail, anchored to the viewport edge. Three sections, stacked: brand wordmark top, nav items middle, userline pinned bottom.
